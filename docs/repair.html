Conditional single-pass repair

Only candidates with concrete problems enter repair.

Local syntax/structure or numeric diagnostics skip the initial judge and become repair feedback. Valid candidates receive a judgment; semantic differences may trigger one repair. Success, uncertainty and malformed judge responses do not trigger speculative rewriting. The complete source and current SOP accompany all observed problems. Generation and repair use the identical dialect prompt file. After repair, local CNL is regenerated and only changed pairs require another judgment. Raw attempts, diagnostics and normalization evidence are preserved.

Cached judgments require an exact source/CNL/notation/anchor pair, the same selected model and the same judge context. Conflicting cached evidence is excluded. Pworker remains generic: these domain rules live in llmFormaliser.
