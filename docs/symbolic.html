Symbolic-first SOP drafts

Two additional experimental task types; the two dialect conventions are shared by every generation and repair phase.

Index

Local discourse draft → local diagnostics → deterministic CNL → cached/fresh judgment for complete drafts → repair only if needed → individual finish.

symbolicformalise_clause-symboliccheck-repair-judge.md and symbolicformalise_event-symboliccheck-repair-judge.md share a newly written, conservative clause parser and have separate SOP exporters. No implementation was copied from nlpFormaliser. The parser recognizes a limited lexical inventory, noun phrases, finite verbs, auxiliary negation/aspect, simple questions/directives, attitude complements and some coordination. Unsupported constructions and unresolved complements remain UM records with source spans in the draft artifact; role and scope uncertainty is reported to the repairer.

A complete candidate reaches judgment first. Accepted candidates never enter repair. Exact accepted cached source/CNL pairs skip both judgment and repair, even when construction had uncertainty notes. Structural failures or unresolved draft diagnostics go directly to repair; a negative semantic judgment also triggers repair. Inconclusive judgments are not grounds for speculative rewriting. The model receives the authoritative source, complete SOP draft, accumulated structural diagnostics, approximate lexical checks and unresolved-fragment notes. Incomplete initial candidates remain unjudged; complete drafts have their own recorded assessment. Final candidates use the existing converters and judge prompt. Incomplete or structurally invalid final results cannot succeed.

begin and draftCheck contain explicit individual code; toCnl converts and checks exact cached judgments; judge uses the best tier; review evaluates each task’s conditions before routing to repair or finish. Repair uses its configured tier and is limited to one pass. No small-tier formalizer is configured or called. All complete tasks are enqueued before one flush. Wave scheduling waits for active phases and local conditions within that run before batching the next cohort. Accepted tasks cannot join repair merely because another task failed.

Artifacts retain the common clause graph, original spans, SOP draft, exact repair input, diagnostics, final SOP/CNL and judgments. Approximate declaration overlap compares literal values, fields, polarity and tense while ignoring reference names. It is not semantic reuse, and empty drafts have undefined retention. More complete modeling of quantifier scope, passive voice, attachments and reference resolution remains outside this conservative prototype.

Domain-specific prompts and converters live in llmFormaliser. Ploinky Workers remains a generic phased executor.
