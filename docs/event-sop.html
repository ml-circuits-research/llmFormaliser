Event-SOP

The following is the exact canonical convention file shared by generation and repair.

Clause-SOP uses grammatical clauses, MOD modifiers and G participant groups. Event-SOP uses semantic roles, proposition graphs, explicit operators and quantifier scope. They share surface syntax but preserve their distinct semantics.

Canonical prompt · Converter · Baseline task

Formalize the entire input in Event-SOP, a typed graph of propositions, semantic roles, scopes and discourse. Output only @id TYPE key=value declarations, one per line, with no JSON objects, prose headings or sentence numbering. The worker may wrap complete SOP strings in its batch results envelope.

Use the explicit field names defined here. $id is a reference; [a,b] is a list. Declare every reference; IDs must be unique across the whole input, not restarted per sentence. Simple literals such as 95%, >500_euro, 09:00 or 2026-04-07 may be bare. Quote whitespace, commas and structural delimiters with matching single or double quotes; escape the delimiter inside the string. Numeric literals may be quoted or bare. Never overload a field: tense describes time, kind describes a record kind, comparison describes a comparison relation. Identical repeated values are harmless. Repeated list-valued roles retain all values; conflicting scalar values need explicit alternatives.

RECORDS
S: exactly one discourse record for the complete input. form=decl|int|imp|excl|frag; act=assert|ask|request|command|suggest|offer|promise|intend|wish|permit|prohibit|define|acknowledge|other. Defaults are decl/assert. This record describes discourse form, not the assertion status of every contained frame. A mixed document has local questions and directives.
E: head, type, name, article, quantifier, count, traits, of, referent, text. head is the lexical noun; name is a proper name; referent=$entity explicitly connects an anaphoric mention. Quantifiers include all,some,none,exact,min,max; quantifier=min count=3 means at least three. article=def means definite, not automatically unique. Keep possessors and restrictions. Separate unrelated situations; connect repeated mentions only when licensed by source context.
EV, ST, RE, EX, ID, DF, CP: event, state, relation, existence, identity, definition and comparison frames. Fields: verb, status, tense, aspect, root, time, place, manner, act. tense=past|present|future; omitted tense is unspecified. status=asserted|unasserted|requested|suggested|offered|promised (as appropriate). An absent status is determined from explicit graph scope; do not mark conditional, queried or reported contents asserted. act can distinguish a request, command or suggestion independently of tense.
Roles: agent, patient, theme, experiencer, stimulus, recipient, beneficiary, tool, source, goal, owner, item, value, standard, topic, content. Roles describe meaning, not grammatical position. Active and passive paraphrases normally share roles. Use owner/item for possession, experiencer/stimulus for experience, and content=$proposition for reported attitudes. Do not put two semantically distinct roles into theme just because there are two nouns.
Multiple participants in one role can use theme=$a theme=$b or theme=[$a,$b]; both retain the same ordered values. When conjunction, alternatives or collective scope matter, declare a G with kind=and|or|alt and members=[...] and reference it. Repeated time/place/manner and content fields also collect values. A tense or status cannot hold conflicting scalar values.
CP: comparison=more|less|equal|superlative|comparative, verb=source_predicate where available, entity/theme, standard, dimension, kind=min|max if needed, plus normal frame fields including tense. comparison and tense are ALWAYS separate. Preserve the comparison target, degree, quantification over alternatives and min/max direction. Do not invent a direction from a missing predicate.
OP: kind, value, scope=$proposition, target(optional), domain, options, cue, scale. Operators are separate records. A frame root=$outermost_operator identifies the full scoped proposition. Operator scope can refer to another operator. Preserve nesting, e.g. required(NOT leave) differs from NOT(required leave). Kinds include negation, modal, epistemic, deontic, ability, focus. Focus values can be exclusive/scalar; target identifies the focused constituent.
L: kind, from=$proposition, to=$proposition. Types include if,unless,only_if,whenever,because,purpose,result,before,after,during,until,contrast,concession,alternative,conjunction,disjunction,content. For if/whenever: from is premise, to is consequence. For unless: from is exception, to is qualified consequence. For only_if: from is the dependent proposition, to is the necessary condition. These links do not assert their endpoints independently. A past because/before/after relation may relate actual events; preserve stated status instead of erasing it. Temporal links alone do not justify inventing occurrence.
ER: kind/verb plus left/right, from/to or owner/item as appropriate; entity relation with explicit participants.
QS: kind=all|some|none etc, entity=$entity, scope=$proposition, order=integer, depends=$outer_quantifier(optional), distribution=distributive|collective. Explicitly model dependency; all results reviewed by a physician need not mean the same physician reviews every result.
LS: kind, scope and members for additional explicit logical scope. Do not replace a scoping relation by a bag of unrelated events.
PS: exists, unique, prior, true, cause, target. These are separate presuppositions; existence alone does not entail uniqueness. Repeated exists/unique/prior/true values are allowed. Preserve source commitments without adding stronger ones.
Q: kind=wh|yn|alternative, target=$proposition_or_operator, answer=person|thing|reason|quantity|selection|truth-value etc, embedded=true(optional). A wh question must retain both the queried relation and the unknown argument, e.g. agent=?who. Do not target an isolated entity when the question asks which entities satisfy a proposition. Question targets and their operator scopes are not independent assertions. Reported questions are embedded; the asking event is separate.
DISC: kind=function span="original wording" target=$proposition(optional). Functions include discourse-marker, approximation, hesitation, emphasis, politeness, stance, emotion and unspecified. Fillers are not automatically emotion. Omitted target means whole-input scope.
UM: span="uninterpreted original wording" reason="unresolved issue" target(optional). Preserve unclear material without pretending that retaining its words establishes its semantics.
AM: kind, span, options=[references], preferred(optional). Represent alternatives as explicit referenced graphs, not inline JSON. CI, CX, MN and MS retain implicature, contextual dependence, mention or missing information using kind, span, target, value and explicit references where needed.

PRESERVATION
Preserve each source proposition, speech act, negation, quantity, time, modal, conditional, exception, comparison, reference, ambiguity and pragmatic marker. Distribute document content across connected records without dropping clauses. A condition is not a fact. A belief is not a fact. A question is not a fact. A directive is not evidence that an action happened. Preserve unknown antecedents and attachments explicitly. Never treat source coverage alone as proof of correct formalization.

EXAMPLES (each is an independent full input)
Text: Mira gave Ren two folders yesterday.
@s1 S
@x1 E head=person name=Mira
@x2 E head=person name=Ren
@x3 E head=folder quantifier=exact count=2
@e1 EV verb=give agent=$x1 recipient=$x2 theme=$x3 tense=past time=yesterday

Text: The green box was lighter than the red box.
@s1 S
@x1 E head=box traits=[green] article=def
@x2 E head=box traits=[red] article=def
@c1 CP verb=light comparison=more theme=$x1 standard=$x2 tense=past

Text: If the lamp flashes, close the hatch.
@s1 S
@x1 E head=lamp article=def
@x2 E head=hatch article=def
@e1 EV verb=flash theme=$x1 status=unasserted
@e2 EV verb=close agent=you theme=$x2 status=requested act=command
@l1 L kind=if from=$e1 to=$e2

Text: Who opened the hatch?
@s1 S form=int act=ask
@x1 E head=hatch article=def
@e1 EV verb=open agent=?who theme=$x1 tense=past
@q1 Q kind=wh target=$e1 answer=person

Text: You must not leave.
@s1 S
@e1 EV verb=leave agent=you root=$o2
@o1 OP kind=negation scope=$e1
@o2 OP kind=deontic value=required scope=$o1

Text: Sol believes that Ren left.
@s1 S
@x1 E head=person name=Sol
@x2 E head=person name=Ren
@e1 EV verb=leave agent=$x2 tense=past
@e2 ST verb=believe experiencer=$x1 content=$e1
