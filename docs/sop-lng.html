Shared SOP syntax

Readable field names with explicit semantics and multiplicity.

@eventId TYPE key=value key=value ...
ConventionMeaning
@id / $idUnique declaration ID / explicit reference. IDs are unique across the entire file; forward references are allowed.
Scalar literalsBare words, numbers, percentages, comparisons, dates and times are accepted. Quote whitespace and structural delimiters. Apostrophes inside bare words are literal. English thousands grouping such as 12,500_euro normalizes to 12500_euro; explicit bracket lists remain lists.
QuotesMatching single or double quotes. Escape the quote delimiter inside its string. Numbers may be quoted or bare.
ListsUse [a,b]. Clause participant groups have their own G declaration. No inline JSON objects.
Identical repeated scalarDeduplicated with recorded normalization; no information is discarded.
List-valued fieldsRepeated values collect in order. Event roles and time/place/manner/content, presuppositions and group members support repetition; Clause traits/flags/items/members support repetition.
Conflicting scalarReported for repair, never silently overwritten. Different tenses need separate events or an explicit alternative.
Missing references / duplicate IDsIdentical repeated declarations normalize idempotently. Conflicting records and missing references remain diagnostics; no meaning is invented.
Field vocabularytense for grammatical time; kind for record type/function; comparison for comparison relation. verb, status, subject, object, agent, theme, scope and other readable names replace ambiguous abbreviations.

The public vocabulary lives in sop-fields.mjs; compact implementation keys are private converter details. Model-facing prompts, symbolic drafts and normalized SOP use readable names. Diagnostics collect independent problems; structure is validated after reliable parsing. Presentation fences can be removed losslessly. Source text is never silently rewritten to force an interpretation.
