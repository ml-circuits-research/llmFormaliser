Formalize the entire input in Clause-SOP: grammatical clauses, explicit references, modifiers and discourse information. Output only SOP declarations, one per line: @id TYPE key=value ... . No JSON objects, prose headings or sentence numbering. The worker may wrap each complete SOP string in its batch results envelope.

Use meaningful field names exactly as defined here. Use $id for references and [a,b] for lists. Simple literals, including 95%, >500_euro, dates and times, may be bare. Quote strings containing whitespace, commas or structural delimiters with matching single or double quotes. Escape a quote inside its own string. Numbers may be quoted or bare. Null means unspecified, never a missing entity. Every $id must be declared. IDs are unique across the ENTIRE input file, not restarted per sentence. Equal repeated fields are harmless. Repeat list fields or give lists; conflicting scalar values need an explicit alternative, not last-value-wins.

RECORDS AND FIELDS
E: quantifier, traits=[...], plural=true|false. Its descriptive ID identifies the noun, e.g. @book-1 E. Use MOD records for possessors, restrictions and other noun properties. Quantifiers may be every, some, no, most, not_all, 3 or ">3". Preserve quantifier dependencies and shared referents across sentences; unrelated situations remain unrelated.
EV: subject, verb, object, status, tense, not, flags, embedded, question, answer, act, formula, logic, items.
subject/object are literals or references. verb is the source lemma; join lexical multiword units with underscores. tense=past|present|future; omitted tense defaults to present. not=true negates this predicate. flags=[PERF,PROG,HABIT,POLITE,EMPH] retain aspect and pragmatic features.
status=asserted (default), unasserted, queried, directive or expressive. Clauses inside conditions, beliefs or reported content must not become independent assertions. status=unasserted preserves their content without asserting it.
For a question use status=queried question=yn|wh|alternative. For wh questions give answer="the requested variable or selection" and preserve ?who, ?what, ?which or ?how_many in the appropriate participant position or modifier. Do not convert which/how-many questions into whether questions. embedded=true distinguishes reported questions.
For directives use status=directive act=request|command|suggest|offer|promise|wish as appropriate. Preserve inclusive suggestions and polite requests. A literal ability question can remain a question; if both readings are unresolved, retain that ambiguity instead of forcing one reading.
For expressive status, object contains the expressed content. For mathematical clauses use formula="ASCII formula". Otherwise use verb+subject, or logic=either|both|not with items=[$event1,$event2]. Exactly one of verb/formula/logic is used in a non-expressive event. For logic, repeat items or supply a list, including singleton [ $event1 ] for not.
MOD: target=$entity_or_event label=relation value=literal_or_reference not=true(optional). One modifier per line. A list-valued participant is represented by a G group, not an inline JSON object. Modifiers can encode mode, time, before, after, by, if, unless, only_if, because, than, of, possession, degree and other source relations. Preserve the difference between at/noon and by/noon.
G: join=and|or|alt|only|even|also members=[literal_or_reference,...]. Nested groups use references. alt retains unresolved readings; never silently choose an antecedent.
DISC: kind=function span="original wording" target=$event(optional). Functions include discourse-marker, approximation, hesitation, emphasis, politeness, stance, emotion, unspecified. Do not classify every filler as emotion. Omitted target means whole-input scope.
UM: span="uninterpreted source wording" reason="unresolved issue" target=$event(optional). Preserve difficult content visibly; retaining words alone does not prove their meaning was formalized.

Repeated traits, flags, items and members collect values. subject/object and tense are scalar: use a G record for multiple or alternative participants. A repeated identical scalar is redundant but accepted. A conflicting scalar remains a diagnostic.

PRESERVATION
Keep negation scope, modal scope, conditions and exceptions, quantities, dates, comparisons, possessors, speech acts and reported content. Preserve ambiguity rather than inventing links. Use explicit MOD/G/EV records to connect content; never use source text to compensate for missing links in the candidate. Reuse the same entity for a definite anaphoric mention only when justified. For independent events with different times, use different EV records rather than conflicting tense values.
